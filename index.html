          WHY GOAL 12?

          The food we already bought is the place to start.

          Households made up 60% of global food waste in 2022, according to the UN. Goal 12.3 is about cutting that waste at the consumer level. The EPA's advice is practical: keep a list of food to use up and plan meals around it. That's what this tool helps you do.

          UN Goal 12 ↗EPA food-waste guide ↗
          A quick food-safety note

          Only use cooked leftovers that were refrigerated promptly and kept cold. USDA guidance says to eat refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days and reheat them to 165°F / 74°C. If you're unsure how something was stored, leave it out.
