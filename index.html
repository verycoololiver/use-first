            04 / THE REASON

            Food waste starts
            before the bin.

            The UN says households produced 60% of the world's food waste in 2022. Sustainable Development Goal 12.3 calls for halving food waste at retail and consumer level by 2030. Making a plan around what is already at home is a small, repeatable way to address that problem.

            The US EPA recommends keeping a list of food to use up and planning meals around it. This tool does exactly that; it does not estimate carbon saved or claim a planned meal was already eaten.

            BEFORE YOU COOK

            Food safety comes first.

            Refrigerate cooked leftovers within two hours—or one hour when it is above 90°F / 32°C—and keep them cold. Use refrigerated leftovers within three to four days and reheat to 165°F / 74°C, checked with a food thermometer. If storage is uncertain or food seems spoiled, do not use it just to avoid waste. This app cannot assess freshness or replace the label and local food-safety guidance.
